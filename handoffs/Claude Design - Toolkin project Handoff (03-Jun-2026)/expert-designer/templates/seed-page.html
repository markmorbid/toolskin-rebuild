Foundations

Three systems do most of the work.

Color from one knob. Type on a 1.200 ladder. Space on the 8pt grid. The rest is taste.

●

One accent knob

Change --ts-accent-h and the entire theme recolors. Surfaces stay; status colors stay; everything else derives.

◐

Locked type ladder

Twelve steps from 11px to 83px. Line-heights and tracking move in lockstep. No off-ladder sizes, ever.

▦

Harmonic spacing

4px base, 8pt grid. Padding ≥ gap. Cards in the same row share a recipe. Layouts don't break under long content.
