ts-accordion variants

Surface: --ts-bg-body · base .ts-accordion (chevron)

The v2 --ts-this-bg engine emits hue-stable surface derivatives (hover, dim, border, focus) via color-mix(in oklab, …). Every component re-anchors --ts-this-bg and the derivatives recompute.
Wave 2 migrates ts-table and ts-accordion onto the v2 engine. Both use the nested CSS-1 pattern; both drop color-mix(in srgb, …), all !important chains, and frozen-only border tokens.
chip in panel accent chip migrated
Both .ts-accordion and .ts-ui-accordion are supported as sibling selectors. BEM elements (__header, __panel, __indicator, etc.) and modifiers (--separated, --dual-icon, --toggle-plus) are also double-listed.

.ts-accordion--separated · spaced bordered cards

Re-anchor --ts-this-bg FIRST, consume derivatives SECOND.
Engine --ts-this-bg-border* only. No currentColor.
No srgb mixes, no HSL, no invented colors.

The indicator rotates the vertical stroke into the horizontal so the plus becomes a minus on expand. Accent recolor on aria-expanded="true".
